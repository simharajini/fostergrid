# FosterGrid — Product Steering

## Mission
Relocate at-risk shelter animals to safe foster homes within minutes during a
crisis (power outage, wildfire, heatwave, flooding).

## Non-negotiable rules
- Never match an animal to a foster inside an active hazard zone.
- Never place a refrigerated-medication animal in a home without backup power.
- Never place a mobility-impaired animal in multi-story housing.
- The deterministic fallback engine must always return a usable result, even if
  Amazon Bedrock is unreachable — the live demo must never show an error.

## Tone
Urgent, clear, trustworthy. This is emergency software for shelter staff under
pressure.
